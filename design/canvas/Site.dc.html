How it works

Four steps, all checked.

The link only appears after the app answers locally and over the public tunnel.

01folder_open

Choose

A Dockerfile, or a Compose file and the web service to share.

02deployed_code

Build and start

Runs in an isolated, app-owned container bound to this computer only.

03verified

Verify

Checks local health, opens a Quick Tunnel, then checks public HTTPS.

04share

Share

Copy or open the link. Hold Stop sharing to end it cleanly.
