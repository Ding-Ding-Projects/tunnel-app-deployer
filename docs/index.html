WHAT THE LINK MEANS

Local hosting, shared openly.

No cloud account

The default tunnel needs no Cloudflare account or custom domain. Your application runs on your computer.

A temporary address

The HTTPS address can change on restart. Your computer, container and tunnel must stay running for the link to work.

Health before success

The app should check local and public reachability before showing a live link, and clear it when sharing stops.

Service limits and availability

Quick Tunnels are intended for development and sharing, have no uptime guarantee, allow up to 200 in-flight requests, and do not support server-sent events. Anyone with the link can access the app; the app's own authentication remains intact.
