# Dim sum surprise

Each time the app opens there is a 1 in 10 chance of a small dim sum note.

The note shows one dish with its English and Cantonese names, waits quietly in the corner, and fades on its own. It never appears during the first-run tour, while hosting is running, or after an error, and it never takes focus.

The switch in **Settings › Appearance** is a joke: turn it off and it turns itself back on.

Pictures of the dishes are planned; the app does not load images from the internet today.
