# Accessibility

Keyboard first, readable at any size, and announced to screen readers.

## Keyboard

Every control can be reached with **Tab**, has a visible focus ring, and works with **Enter** or **Space**. The command palette (**Ctrl+Shift+F**) reaches every screen, action and setting. **Shift+F10** opens the context menu of the focused item.

## Size and density

- **Text size** scales the whole interface from 90 to 150 percent.
- **Density** switches between comfortable and compact spacing.
- Layouts are designed down to 420 pixels wide; nothing is cut off, and long addresses wrap.

## Screen readers

Progress messages, copied links and notifications are announced through a polite live region. Icon-only buttons have names. Errors use an alert role.

## Contrast and motion

Colours are generated to keep text at a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 in both themes. Motion follows your system's reduced-motion setting.
