# School mode

A simple, focused version of the app for classrooms.

While School mode is on, the app uses English only, plain wording, no emoji, no personal vocabulary and no dim sum surprise. Those settings are left out of Settings and the command palette instead of being shown disabled.

## Turning it on and off

Turn it on in **Settings › Language and voice** and choose a PIN of 4 to 12 digits. Turning it off asks for that PIN. The PIN is stored on this computer as a salted SHA-256 hash.

School mode is a convenience lock, not a security boundary: clearing the app's data on this computer resets it.

## Not built yet

Sharing one School mode switch across several apps on the same computer is planned.
