# Appearance

Material Design 3 colours, shapes and type, all generated from one accent colour.

## Theme

**Follow system**, **Light** or **Dark**. The button in the top bar flips between light and dark.

## Accent colour

Drag the hue slider, press a swatch, or type a hex value such as `#3ddbb4`. The app builds its whole colour scheme from it: primary, containers, surfaces and outlines in both light and dark. Text colours are chosen to keep a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.

## Navigation rail

**Expanded** writes each destination's name beside its icon and turns the New host button into a labelled button. **Compact** shows icons with small labels underneath. Switch with the menu button at the top of the rail or in this group. Windows narrower than 1100 pixels always use compact.

## Window

On Windows the app draws its own title bar, with the app name, version, dim sum code name and build time. The minimise, maximise and close buttons are Windows' own, so Snap layouts work as usual. Drag the title bar to move the window. Other systems keep their normal window frame.

## Type and icons

Text uses Roboto Flex, addresses and timings use Roboto Mono, and icons come from Material Symbols Rounded. All three are bundled, so the app never loads fonts from the internet.

## What is not built yet

Editing the look of a single element and translating colours between colour spaces are planned.
