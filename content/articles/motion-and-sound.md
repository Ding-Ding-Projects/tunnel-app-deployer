# Motion and sound

Movement explains what changed. Sound confirms what happened. Both can be turned down or off.

## Motion levels

- **Full**: cards rise in, the link card grows into place, the tunnel line flows while sharing, and a failure gives one short shake.
- **Gentle**: shorter, smaller movements and no looping animation.
- **Off**: instant changes. This is also used whenever your system asks for reduced motion, whatever you choose here.

Motion never carries meaning on its own. Every state also changes its text, colour or icon.

## Action sounds

Short tones play when hosting starts, when the link goes live, when sharing stops and when hosting fails. They are made by the app itself, so no audio files are loaded. Turn them off or set the volume in **Settings › Motion and sound**. The setting applies at once and is remembered.
