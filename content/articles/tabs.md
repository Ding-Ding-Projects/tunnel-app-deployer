# Tabs

Keep a few screens one click away.

Ordinary navigation never opens tabs. The **Workspace** tab follows the rail as usual. You open extra tabs yourself:

- Press **Ctrl+T**, or select **+** in the tab strip, to open the New tab catalogue.
- The catalogue lists screens, tools, settings groups and guide articles. Type to filter it.
- Choose an entry to open it in a new tab.

## Keys

- **Ctrl+Tab** and **Ctrl+Shift+Tab** move between tabs.
- **Ctrl+W** closes the current tab. The Workspace tab cannot be closed.
- **Enter** or **Space** opens a focused tab.

Open tabs are remembered between sessions. The strip is hidden while only the Workspace tab is open.
