# Notifications

Small messages appear in the corner and never block what you are doing.

## Toasts

Success and information messages appear at the bottom and fade after the time you choose in **Settings › Notifications** (3 to 15 seconds). Errors and warnings stay until you dismiss them. Several toasts stack without covering each other.

## The notification centre

Press the bell in the top bar. Every notification is kept there, newest first, with its time. Search it (with the regex builder), select several to dismiss them, export them as JSON, or **Clear all…** with the two-key confirmation. The bell's badge counts unread notifications.
