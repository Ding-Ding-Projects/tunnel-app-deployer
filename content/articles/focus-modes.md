# Focus modes

Five separate switches that make the app easier to follow. All are off until you turn them on.

| Mode | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Focus | Hides secondary notes, the requirements bar and the footer. |
| Low stimulation | Softer colours and no motion. Your system's reduced-motion setting still applies on top. |
| Time awareness | A clock in the top bar, and how long the current step has been running. |
| One thing at a time | Host shows only the card for the step you are on. |
| Momentum | A single line under the top bar names the next action. |

The modes are plain tools. They keep no streaks or scores and make no medical claims.
