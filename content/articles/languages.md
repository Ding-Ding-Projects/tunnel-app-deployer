# Languages and voice

The app and the website speak English, Hong Kong Cantonese, or both side by side.

## Language modes

- **English**.
- **廣東話**: written Hong Kong Cantonese in traditional characters.
- **Bilingual**: the English label stays first, with the Cantonese label smaller beside or under it. Narrow windows wrap the second label instead of squeezing it.

Change it in **Settings › Language and voice**, or in the first-run tour. The choice is saved on this computer; the website saves its own choice in your browser.

## Playfulness

Two sliders, one for English and one for Cantonese, go from **1 (serious)** to **5 (playful)**. They change the voice of headings, notifications and empty states. Facts never change: an error still names the same cause and the same fix at every level. Both ship at 5 and reset separately.

## Emoji in dialogs

When turned on, confirmations and notifications get one small emoji. Emoji never appear in buttons, labels or the names read by screen readers.
