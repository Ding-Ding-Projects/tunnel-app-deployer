# Getting around

Six destinations, one search bar, and a menu on everything you can right-click.

## Destinations

- **Host**: choose a project and share it.
- **Sessions**: every hosting attempt on this computer.
- **Tools**: a Base64 toolkit and a table converter.
- **Features**: what the app does, with an honest status for each part.
- **Guide**: these articles, bundled inside the app so they open offline.
- **Settings**: appearance, motion, language, focus modes and data.

In a wide window they sit in the navigation rail on the left. Below 860 pixels they move to a bar at the bottom.

## Context menus

Right-click a link, a path, a session or selected text, or press **Shift+F10** on the focused item. Each menu starts with a filter field: type to narrow it. Menus show the shortcut for each item.

Settings appear in menus with their live control beside the label: a switch for an on or off setting, and choice buttons for a setting with options. Right-click any setting row to get its own control at the top. Change them in place, and the menu stays open: **Space** or **Enter** flips a switch, and **Left** and **Right** move between choices. Every menu also ends with **Quick settings** for theme, motion, action sounds, focus and the narrator.

## Panels

The notification centre, command palette and confirmations open over the screen. **Escape** closes the top one and focus returns to where you were.

## The offline guide

Every article on the website ships inside the app. Links between articles stay inside the app, and the guide has its own search with the regex builder.
